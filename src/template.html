BEFORE YOU BEGIN

A few clear answers.

What is available, what is saved, and what this experience can offer.

Which reading should I choose?

Tarot explores a specific situation. Astrology explores themes in your birth chart. You can begin with either.

What do reversed cards mean?

Reversed does not mean bad. It can suggest a theme that is blocked, quieter or changing. Choose whether to include reversals before shuffling.

Can I draw another card?

You can add one clarification card for a specific question. It adds context and keeps the original cards in place.

What if I do not know my birth time?

Select “I do not know”. We show the planetary themes we can establish, explain uncertainties, and leave out the Ascendant and houses.

Do I need a subscription?

No. Readings and your notebook are available without a paid membership. AI readings may have usage limits.

Where are my readings saved?

Saved readings stay in this browser. When you request an AI reading, your question and relevant information are processed online. Clearing browser data removes local records.

Do I need to buy anything?

No. The collection is optional and is currently a preview. No object changes or cancels a reading.
