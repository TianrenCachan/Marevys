BEFORE YOU BEGIN

A few clear answers.

What is available, what is saved, and what this experience can offer.

Which reading should I choose?

Choose Runes to isolate one priority for the next seven days, Tarot to see a three-part weekly pattern, or the I Ching to examine structure and timing across a larger 3–12 month change.

What is a rune reading?

You blind-draw one of the 24 Elder Futhark characters. MARÉVYS places its historical identity inside a modern seven-day framework: supporting force, blocked or excessive expression, warning signs and one controlled action.

Are the stones drawn blind? Do runes have upright and reversed positions?

Yes. The rune experience reshuffles all 24 stones and asks you to blind-draw one. MARÉVYS does not assign upright or reversed positions: every rune is considered through both its central meaning and its shadow edge.

Is the reading free? Do I need an account?

The current reading preview is free and needs no account to begin. The personal archive demonstrates a future member feature: sign in or join CERCLE to reopen past readings.

Are Tarot cards selected blind? Are reversed cards used?

Yes. The 22 Major Arcana remain face down until you select them. This edition does not use reversed cards; each card is read through both its central expression and its shadow or caution.

How is an I Ching hexagram formed?

You cast three virtual coins six times, placing the lines from bottom to top. The primary hexagram shows the present structure; changing lines locate the turning conditions; the relating hexagram shows a conditional direction rather than a fixed final outcome.

Can I buy the products?

Not in this preview. The collection shows product concepts, not final offers for sale. You can browse details and save a wishlist; ordering, payment and delivery are not available.

Where are my readings and wishlist saved?

In this preview they remain only in this browser on this device. The member gate demonstrates future access but does not create a cloud account or synchronization. Clearing browser data or using a private session may remove them.

How does energy matching work?

MARÉVYS connects the context of your question with the themes in your chosen symbolic system, then composes four sensory objects into one practical ritual. For the full explanation, see the Reading notice.

Can I save Tarot and I Ching readings?

Yes. Completed readings from all three systems can be saved on this device with your reflection. Reopening the archive passes through the CERCLE member preview.
