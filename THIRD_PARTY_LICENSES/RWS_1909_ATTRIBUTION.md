# Rider–Waite–Smith card faces

78 original card faces from the 1909 **Roses & Lilies** edition of the Rider–Waite–Smith Tarot. Artwork by **Pamela Colman Smith (1878–1951)**, conceived with Arthur Edward Waite, first published by William Rider & Son. These scans were made by **Saskia Jansen** from her personal collection and are hosted by Wikimedia Commons.

Source collection: [Wikimedia Commons — Roses & Lilies](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Rider-Waite_tarot_deck_(Roses_%26_Lilies)). Each card's description page, original URL, source SHA-1, and output SHA-256 are recorded in `RWS_1909_PROVENANCE.json`.

## Files and order

- `tarotClassic00.webp` through `tarotClassic21.webp`: Major Arcana, Fool through World; Strength is 08 and Justice is 11.
- `tarotClassic22.webp` through `tarotClassic35.webp`: Wands.
- `tarotClassic36.webp` through `tarotClassic49.webp`: Cups.
- `tarotClassic50.webp` through `tarotClassic63.webp`: Swords.
- `tarotClassic64.webp` through `tarotClassic77.webp`: Pentacles.
- Each suit is ordered Ace, Two through Ten, Page, Knight, Queen, King.

The finished WebP files are proportionally resized to 500 pixels wide, without cropping, color changes, or retouching. All source detail, including original English lettering, remains in the images. Their natural aspect ratio is approximately 0.58; use containment instead of cropping when displaying them in a different card ratio. French and Chinese names should appear as interface text.

The `originals/` folder contains the downloaded JPEGs for reproducibility. Contact sheets are review artifacts. Only the 78 WebP images and whichever attribution/provenance files the site needs should be copied into its production asset directory.
